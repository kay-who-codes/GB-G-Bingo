# GB&G Bingo - Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 18 October 2026*

GB&G Bingo is a Discord Activity. **The developer does not collect, receive, store, or have access to any player data or sessions.** There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no server that receives anything from you.

## What stays on your device

The game saves your bingo card, your sound setting, and your author filter in your own browser's local storage so they persist between visits. This data never leaves your device and the developer cannot see it. Clearing your browser or Discord data deletes it. 

## What data is collected

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Discord and hosting

The Activity runs inside Discord, which handles your account and voice session under [Discord's Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy). The files are served by a web host, which may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) under its own policy. The developer does not access those logs for this app.
